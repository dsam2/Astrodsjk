Understanding Your Kundali

Lagna & Planetary Positions

The chart opens with your ascendant and the sign, degree, and nakshatra of every graha. House placement is where most interpretation begins.

Rashi (D1) and Navamsa (D9)

The D1 shows the broad layout of your life, while the Navamsa (D9) carries the deeper strength of a planet and a great deal of marriage and dharma indication.

Vimshottari Dasha

The timing backbone of a Parashari reading. It marks the period you are in now, down through Mahadasha, Antardasha, and finer levels.

Yogas & Jaimini Karakas

Recognised planetary combinations (Yogas) and soul significators (Chara Karakas) like the Atmakaraka are calculated to understand temperament and life direction.
